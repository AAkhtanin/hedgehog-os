The central evaluation question is not whether a model sounds persuasive. It is whether an intention or permitted change alters actual Work, whether the resulting material is consumed, and whether historical facts and current decisions remain distinguishable. A positive outcome is paired with a meaningful adverse or changed-condition neighbor. Useful continuation matters as much as refusal: a system that rejects everything would not demonstrate the intended organization of activity.

Five Evidence Classes

Accepted contracts define a finite profile's obligations. Recorded execution shows what particular code and inputs did. Independent checking can compare source-bound values or review an execution under stated trust assumptions. Architectural inference explains a broader relationship supported by several cases. Proposed future work is not execution. The editorial map records these classes rather than decorating every case with a green tick.

Causal Evidence Is More Than a Successful Report

For a consumed result, the useful chain is actual input, producer result, wrapper/currentness material, downstream input and outcome. Football compares parent-held inputs and observed wire material with native Work; Wedding points to USED field references and retained output values; EWS connects the affected source and retained photo snapshot to the recomputed result. A candidate's self-report is not its own independent oracle.

The Incident-to-Proof Atlas makes a further distinction. DIRECT_CAUSAL cases connect the boundary event to later consumption. SHARED_DOMAIN_PROOF cases test another obligation on a common domain consumer. POST_CONSUMER_CONTROL cases happen after that consumer and cannot be its cause. Twenty cards are not twenty independent learners. Their source clocks, observations and native meanings must remain visible.

What Has Been Rechecked Here

This corpus performs data-only source selection, field comparison, link/format validation and editorial review. It does not rerun signatures, Football, Wedding hardware, the complete Gate6 verifier or any provider. Counts of inspected fields are publication QA, not new functional acceptance. Historical timing is reported with its measured scope: fewer domain executor calls does not by itself establish faster wall time, and nested inclusive timings cannot be added as independent cost shares.

The reproducibility route begins with inert saved objects. Pure supplied verification is a second route that consumes saved data and writes verification results. Fresh deterministic native execution is a third route with current inputs and dependencies. Live execution adds separately authorized network credentials, a provider/model and budget. These routes establish different things. A package manifest checked against itself proves consistency, not independent provenance; a reader needs a separately trusted commit or manifest digest.

Boundaries Are Testable Assumptions

Reviewed reference code, the observer and the Host/Firewall may share a process within the declared profile. That is a trust assumption to state, not a reason to pretend an unimplemented external action service exists. Conversely, an achieved bypass through an in-scope input remains a finding even if it reaches trusted code. The audit boundary cannot be moved after the fact to dismiss the result.

These finite observations do not certify production security, arbitrary-program isolation or universal intent handling. They do support an inspectable account of useful work across different tasks, participants, times and owners. The chapters preserve failures and lawful neighbors so that the reader can judge the actual construct rather than a curated sequence of success labels.
