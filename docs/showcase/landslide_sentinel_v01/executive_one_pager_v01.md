# Landslide Sentinel

## Local responsibility. Replaceable intelligence.

Radiolaria OS composes a monitoring cell around current evidence. A reasoning model can propose a useful investigation; the runtime binds its inputs and actual work, while local Root decisions and the live Host control every admitted effect. The demonstration is implemented and committed in `main` at `2f328be634247be11bc18a3b22a919f393d6ed1d`.

## The demonstrated lifecycle

The synthetic S0-S5 story configures monitoring, responds to changed rainfall, investigates local observations, completes a local mock signal while optional reasoning remains pending, delivers an immutable report, and clears the incident through measured recovery and a distinct OFF action.

In the latest live experiment, identical readings with different maintenance questions produce different work: current-record metadata checks versus an admitted reserve-history read measuring three samples over 20 seconds with a 150-micrometre range. Three genuine Gemini contributions also reach one common C/D/Work/Root composition. Their choices and resulting work are recorded; earlier inconclusive pairs remain preserved.

## Measured, with scopes intact

The latest live stage took **205.70 seconds**, including **100.55 seconds for D** and **five Gemini calls totalling 67.42 seconds**. A separate current-policy experiment recorded **242.59 seconds for E** and demonstrated an actual 60-second next monitoring interval. These are overlapping components of different experiments, not additive benchmarks or response-time guarantees.

An independent historical Environment Agency acquisition returned **eight real zero-valued rainfall readings**. Unknown interval bounds and missing physical mapping were retained. Zero observations were not substituted for absent data.

## What the evidence establishes

- Meaning changes actual work; citations do not become acquisition or action authority.
- Current policy and measured time govern continuation and recovery.
- A fresh local invocation can act before an older optional response returns.
- LocalDRS reuses reviewed meaning under current eligibility, without restoring old permission.
- Safe-derived replay checks exact bytes and supported relationships against an independently supplied pin; three substitution controls are refused.

Source landing preserves **1,015 protected base files**, adds or updates **46 files**, and retains **nine Living registrations**. Recorded push and clean-tree checks are included in the return.

## Scope and limits

Physical-domain effects are local mocks. Gemini is actual cloud transport emulating an intended internal SLM role; installed local-model hardware was not demonstrated. Physical calibration, gauge-to-slope relevance, prediction, production timing and distributed exactly-once delivery are not established. Native historical-graph replay remains unsupported. The presentation and standalone XML reader are explanatory derivatives with source identities, not new authority or an extension of the frozen evidence seal.
