# Cover artwork provenance

`radiolaria_reference_cover.png` is a new conceptual brand illustration generated
for the Gate 5 publication at the owner's request on 26 September 2026 (Europe/Tirane).
It replaces the artwork reused in the earlier publication preparation.

The direction follows the established Radiolaria showcase style: a white field,
smoky silver silica-like openwork, a floating specimen, exactly eight principal
rays and small, barely visible blue-violet details. The previous Gate 3, Gate 4
and G54B1 illustrations provide the visual family, not scientific evidence.

The asset appears once, on the title slide, at its original proportions.
Image dimensions: 1672 by 941 pixels. SHA-256:
`a8570e18d4c79834fb188e8d24cf0612380a5430bf370676bf69421ffb1c49f6`.

The image-generation call produced publication artwork only. It was not a new
authoring experiment, provider trial, runtime action or Gate 5 engineering run.
The specimen is neither a graph of the system nor captured scientific or execution
evidence. It makes no claim about a biological species.
