# Testflix: a short demonstration story

> English translated edition of the historical V09 story. The original outcomes, measurements and limitations are retained. This translation does not claim a new execution or final acceptance.

All amounts below are in minor currency units. The configured Gemini responses,
their local bindings to requests, computation, Root review and evidence are real.
The catalog and time are controlled; purchases and devices are mocks.

1. The customer prefers AD_FREE with a limit of 650. The interpreter records
   the intent, the analyst examines catalog terms, the selector proposes the
   500 plan, and the reviewer independently checks the proposal. Their distinct
   actual outputs are visible in the viewer's expandable records. UserRoot,
   BankRoot, ProviderRoot and DeviceRoot each decide independently; playback
   starts after native execution.
2. An informational request uses retained admissible results. Stop and a fresh
   session do not become a repeated purchase and do not change Hosts.
3. In controlled P04, an actual quote-bound packet for 500 is already waiting.
   A new price of 700 is observed later; trusted current evaluation is also
   later, while the original packet TTL remains valid. The old packet is refused
   before the effect. The common public E recomputes the affected branch in
   318.0 s and preserves the unaffected paid period. A new BankRoot review
   refuses under the 650 limit.
4. An independent 600 branch actually completes another E in 318.9 s, receives
   a new review/packet and makes one mock payment. This is not a third payment
   in the main story. Coherent N15 mutations and incorrect time/binding are
   refused; legitimate equivalent inputs pass.
5. At exact expiry, the old right is not used as a current one. New explicit
   consent for 700 starts a separate renewal. Live roles actually consume the
   updated terms and limit; new Root decisions and a receipt then establish
   a new paid period. Stop/fresh-session within that period add no payment.
6. The main story ends with the same four Hosts and two mock payments. Seal and
   offline replay check the old/new history without provider/effect calls and
   without deriving current permission from memory.
7. The first A/B ended in actual role disagreement. The owner authorized
   additional requests; the common QUALITY input contract was clarified without
   weakening refusal predicates. Two new intent/terms responses are retained,
   but the selector received two Google504 errors, followed by httpx.ReadTimeout
   after the server/client deadline was increased to180s. Public input-bound
   resume and the SDK server-deadline were checked; no result was invented.
   Positive A/B consumption is NOT complete; the exact transport errors are retained.
8. The captured-handler executes the same scenario again using new actual
   Hosts, retained input-bound responses and 0 LLM calls. The other canonical
   history fields match; four explicit mode fields differ in provenance meaning.
   This is new mock execution, not merely viewing a seal.

One early actual model refusal is retained: an unclear initial task projection
caused a request for additional data. After clarifying the task/units, the
validator was not weakened; that response is still refused. There were
18 attempts/15 responses in total, with 13 accepted role outputs.

`story.html` opens without a server or network. The complete live story took
19.6 minutes; the model does not account for the main local cost of history
validation. This is a finite checked example, not universal or production
certification. Independent review/admission/owner landing remain.
