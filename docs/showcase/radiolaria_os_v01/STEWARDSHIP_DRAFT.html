Project Stewardship

I want Radiolaria to grow beyond what one person can maintain. I welcome teams building on the published kernel under its applicable licenses. I am also open to discussing a strategic acquisition of the technology and the intellectual property rights I hold by an organization able to develop it further.

My aim is to make a clear technical handover and return to research, rather than build a consultancy or promise indefinite personal support. Any acquisition or transfer would require a separate agreement. This statement does not change the licenses already granted for published material.
