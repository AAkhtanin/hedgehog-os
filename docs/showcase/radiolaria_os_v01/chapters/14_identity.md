# A Common Kernel for Continuing Work

The examples expose a common way of organizing application work. Intent is related to the computation actually performed; results retain their provenance; changing conditions affect their current use; and decisions remain with the relevant local owners. The achieved object is therefore broader than a seating solver, a booking script or a filter around model output, while remaining bounded by the implemented profiles.

## Evidence and Its Visible Companion

A recognizable visual representation can help identify an evidence object. The Visual Seal concept relates a concrete object to its digest and then, through a versioned visual profile, to a visible companion. The image does not independently prove truth, origin, permission or currentness. It is distinct from a cryptographic evidence seal and is not a reverse decoder.

The inherited B1R3 preview and SVG are NONCANONICAL prototypes. Their concrete receipt-to-image input and manifest are not established in this corpus. ## Technical Conclusion

Across the finite reference cases, semantic interpretation contributes to actual Work; computed results cross typed boundaries; useful retained work survives selected changes; and renewed local checks determine whether material is applicable now. Independent owners can cooperate without a superior owner. Experience can alter inspection or allocation without becoming permission. External authors can supply a component without acquiring the right to admit it or approve its future actions. [W-W4](../PAPER_SOURCE_KEY.md#w-w4), [W-Q3](../PAPER_SOURCE_KEY.md#w-q3), [WS-P_temporal](../PAPER_SOURCE_KEY.md#ws-p_temporal), [WS-P_consumption](../PAPER_SOURCE_KEY.md#ws-p_consumption), [E05](../PAPER_SOURCE_KEY.md#e05), [E08](../PAPER_SOURCE_KEY.md#e08), [X-A-REPORT](../PAPER_SOURCE_KEY.md#x-a-report), [R-AUTHOR](../PAPER_SOURCE_KEY.md#r-author)

For integrators, this common organization supplies explicit roles for domain knowledge, numerical methods, source observations, validation and effect adapters. Existing tools can contribute their particular capabilities while the task retains an inspectable account of what was used and who decided. A new domain still requires its own contracts, engineering, independent checks and operational safeguards. The reference does not establish zero integration cost, arbitrary-code isolation or unmeasured scale. [R-CONTRACT](../PAPER_SOURCE_KEY.md#r-contract), [R-KIT](../PAPER_SOURCE_KEY.md#r-kit)

The evidence remains deliberately specific: two fresh and nine retained profiles, 69 satisfied obligations and four approved deferments, with controlled, captured, historical live-origin and mock-effect records distinguished. No universal security, hardware advantage or production certification follows. Within that boundary, Radiolaria offers a concrete kernel and source-linked examples for applications in which intelligence helps carry work forward without owning its consequences.
