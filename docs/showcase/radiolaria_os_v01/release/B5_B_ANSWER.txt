1. **Q1 — What Radiolaria organizes.** Radiolaria organizes continuing application activity: semantic proposals, programs, solvers, services, retained results, changing conditions and decisions by local owners. RDL Kernel supplies the common execution and authority relationships; integrators supply domain profiles, schemas, capabilities, adapters and policies. It is an application execution kernel, not a bootable OS replacement. Its purpose extends beyond filtering model output because it creates bounded Work, binds actual producer outputs to consumers, supports continuation and reuse, and controls consequential effects.  
   **Sources:** `publication/chapters/01_intent.md`, “From Intent to Governed Activity” and “What OS Means Here” (derived explanation); `publication/references/current/current_architecture_lock_v01.md` §§3–7 (accepted normative source; §§3–7 also form excerpt EV001).

3. **Q3 — Distinct boundaries.** Models and orchestrators **propose** structure; runtime constructs execution topology and invokes **bounded Work**. Checking or accepting a result does not authorize an action: consequential effects require a current, scoped Root-created `ActionCommitPacket` and the exclusive Firewall/Corridor path. **Examining new code** tests a candidate against independent requirements and observations. **Admitting code** is a separate owner-authorized operation on reviewed source bytes; admission supplies no future runtime permission.

   Child Work remains bounded by contract, scope and budget; nesting creates neither additional authority nor a Root for every child. Independent owners exchange evidence and make their own decisions, with no SuperRoot. Permission from A to B does not automatically let B grant A’s rights to C.  
   **Sources:** architecture lock §§3–7; `publication/chapters/02_geometry.md`, “Delegating Work Does Not Delegate Final Authority”; accepted explanatory dossier EV048, `publication/references/accepted/docs/showcase/gate5_reference_v01/dossiers/A.md` §§A10–A11.
