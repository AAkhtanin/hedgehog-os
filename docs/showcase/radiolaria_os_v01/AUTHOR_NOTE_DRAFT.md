# From the Author

I started Radiolaria with a simple question: could a computer organize its work around what a person wants to accomplish, instead of making that person move the task between separate tools? That question guided the kernel, its applications and the way this release is presented.

Over roughly four months, I developed the project as its sole human author, working closely with large language models. I set the architecture, chose the direction and made the final decisions. The models helped write code, examine alternatives, test implementations and prepare the documentation. I remain responsible for what I publish.

This release marks the limit of what I can sustainably take forward alone, not the limit of the architecture. I want other developers, researchers and organizations to use the kernel for real integrations and build the capabilities they need. I am not planning to turn this into a business built around my personal availability for every implementation. I also want to return to the research that led me here.

The way the project was developed is also a way to explore it. You can read it yourself or work through it with your own LLM. The explanations, diagrams and source files are linked so that you can move from an idea to its implementation and recorded evidence. I would rather give you the means to examine the work than ask you to take my word for it.

Akhtanin Andrii
