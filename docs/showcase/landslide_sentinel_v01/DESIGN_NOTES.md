# Landslide Sentinel presentation design

## Shared visual identity

The Ephemeral Workspace deck establishes a white field, Inter typography, black text, quiet blue accents and a silver/white radiolarian sculpture with cobalt detail. Landslide Sentinel retains this family. Its new organism has a wider porous shell, stratified equatorial ribs and six long radial spines. The form is conceptual brand artwork, not a biological classification, hardware topology or physical sensing result. Generation provenance is supplied with the asset.

## Changes from the preceding deck

The narrative expands from 25 to 36 slides. More space is devoted to causality: model input and output, BSEP transfer, actual Work consumption, the note-only A/B comparison, pending-cloud ordering, current-policy cadence and measured recovery. Editable native sequences show event order; the A/B comparison includes a native chart of the three recorded mock-history values. Detailed source identities sit in speaker notes and the technical appendix.

The appendix and single-file reader retain depth that would overload a projected slide. The reader includes full matching D/E runtime bodies as well as current domain sources. An offline HTML story makes the same narrative readable on narrow screens and reveals technical notes without shrinking body text.

## Presentation grammar

- White canvas, restrained cobalt accents and flat ruled tables.
- Short technical titles, direct causal language and scoped measurements.
- Separate historical, controlled and live observations.
- Editable native slide objects for text, tables and data.
- Evidence identities in notes, with companion machine-readable mappings.
- No new scientific result inferred from the artwork, charts or publication polish.

The presentation assets have their own byte identities. They do not extend the frozen LS2R2 package seal or its independent pin.
